# SLC (Student Life Centre): visual reference notes

Sources: 6 Commons photos, dated 2007 to 2020 (see `../ATTRIBUTION.csv`).

**Important:** the SLC was expanded in the 2020s (the SLC/PAC expansion). None of these photos shows the expansion, and no freely licensed photo of it was found. The newest photo here is June 2020 (`SLC_east_pergola_walkway_QNC_beyond_2020.jpg`).

Orientation from OSM: SLC spans about lat 43.4712 to 43.4728 and lon -80.5458 to -80.5444. MC is directly east, QNC south-east, and PAC west. I could not confirm which compass direction the courtyard faces from these photos.

## Courtyard (crescent) facade: `SLC_courtyard_panorama_2007.jpg`, `SLC_courtyard_curved_facade_2012.jpg`, `SLC_courtyard_night_2009.jpg`
- **Plan.** A concave crescent wraps a paved semicircular courtyard. The crescent is about 3 storeys.
- **Glazing.**
  - Ground floor: tall blue-tinted glass in dark mullions, slightly faceted around the curve, with deep grey sill/plinth panels at the bottom.
  - Level 2: a continuous ribbon of square-ish blue glass panes with a silver/grey metal spandrel band above and below.
  - Level 3: a further ribbon band set back, carried around the curve by a light silver metal fascia.
  - Pane rhythm on the 2nd floor: about 30 to 35 panes along the visible arc (approximate).
- **Doors.** At least **2 sets of double glass doors** are recessed in the curved ground-floor glazing: one near the left third and one right of centre (`SLC_courtyard_curved_facade_2012.jpg`). There are further doors under each end pergola.
- **End pavilions.** Both ends of the crescent finish in **buff/tan brick or split-face block pavilions** (about `#a07a55`) with flat roofs and few windows. A thin glass corner slot appears at the far left in 2007.
- **Roof additions.** Behind the crescent at upper left is a **grey horizontally ribbed metal-clad box** (mechanical penthouse) with a louvre. There is also a flat-roofed brick block.
- **Pergolas.** At each end, in front of the pavilion entrances, stands a **timber pergola** with a slatted, sloped timber roof. Its posts are tapered buff concrete-block or split-face piers, and stainless guy rods or cables run to a mast. Structurally these are slatted canopies, not climbing trellises.
- **Courtyard ground.**
  - Light grey square concrete pavers.
  - **Dark charcoal paver bands** in a grid and radial pattern; one band leads straight out from the centre.
  - A central **raised round concrete planter** with a single small tree (honey locust-like) and grasses.
  - Smaller planting pockets with perennials.
  - Low curved concrete seat walls with steel handrails on the ramped edges (left and right in 2007).
  - Steps down at the right edge.
- **Furniture.**
  - 2007: **maroon/burgundy metal picnic tables** with perforated tops and attached seats.
  - 2012: **timber-topped picnic tables** on metal frames.
  - Grey square litter bins sit beside the pergola piers.
  - A cast iron drainage grate is in the foreground.

## East side pergola walkway (toward QNC): `SLC_east_pergola_walkway_QNC_beyond_2020.jpg`
- A long covered walkway runs beside the building. It is a timber slat pergola (diagonal lattice of slats on deep timber beams) on **tapered, battered buff split-face block piers**, about 7 piers visible.
- Timber-slat benches sit on concrete feet between the piers. There is a low block seat with a **red** seat top, and a block seat-wall/planter.
- The paving is light concrete with a darker grey paver band.
- At right is the SLC corner: buff block wall plus full-height dark-framed glazing about 3 storeys tall, with a pendant lamp inside, and a galvanised bike rack.
- Trees: tall blue spruce and deciduous trees (catalpa-like large leaves at left). QNC's finned bar and rooftop stacks show in the background.

## Bombshelter patio (pub): `SLC_bombshelter_patio_night_2013.jpg`
- Night photo taken during a temporary ski-ramp event, so the snow ramp and wooden scaffolding are not permanent.
- It does show: a dark brick wall with a lit serving window, a glass door, timber picnic tables, and wall-mounted floodlights. Low detail.

## Details
- `SLC_land_acknowledgement_plaque_2018.jpg`: a black/bronze rectangular plaque with raised gold letters on **red-brown brick**, laid in running bond. Commons places it at the SLC, but the exact wall is unknown. The brick here is red-brown, darker than the buff courtyard block, so it is probably on a different (older) part of the building.
