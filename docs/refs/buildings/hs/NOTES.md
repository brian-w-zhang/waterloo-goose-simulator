## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

Images in `private/web/` (git-ignored), sources in `private/web/web.jsonl`. Compass sides are inferred from context, not verified against the footprint; check against Street View/Earth captures.

7 photos, 2010-2020s (2017 then-and-now, Commons 2012, Flickr 2010-2014, Daily Bulletin 2014, uwaterloo banner).

- Original 1968 Raymond Moriyama building (pond side): low 1-2 storeys in pale/white stucco or concrete, with angled sloped roof planes and dormer-like skylight monitors. Horizontal glazing bands sit just above the water's edge. Seen across the pond in web_02, web_04 (night) and the silhouettes web_03/05. The 1968 B&W print in web_01 shows the same massing reflected in the pond.
- 2014 addition (Kearns Mancini with John MacDonald): a dark grey metal-panel box with a full-height glazed upper storey cantilevers over a white V-shaped column. It also has a board-formed concrete wall, corrugated/ribbed grey metal cladding on an adjacent wing, and a glazed ground floor (web_10). A green roof is reported but not seen.
- Main entrance (web_11, 2020s): beige/warm-grey metal panels over a glazed storefront. A black pylon carries a yellow-accent "HS" sign, beside a buff stucco wall and a crabapple tree.
- Missing: the street/drop-off side in full, the car and bus drop-off loop, the roof, and the old-to-new junction. The pond-side shots are mostly silhouettes, so material detail is limited.
