## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

3 kept in `private/web/` (list in `web.jsonl`). These cover V1 and all of its houses (VE/VN/VS/VW 1-6 folders). No photos could be tied to a single house.

- **Massing:** long, low brown-brick wings, 2-3 storeys, arranged around quads. Taller, windowless brick stair and vent towers rise above the rooflines at intervals and give a stepped skyline (`uw-v1-hero`). The roofs are flat.
- **Materials:** dark chocolate-brown brick throughout, with darker metal-framed windows in narrow vertical strips. Window rhythm: vertical glazed bays between broad brick piers, with no horizontal banding.
- **Courtyard (`yt-75DD…`, winter):** grey concrete walks with a ramp and steel pipe rail. There are timber picnic tables with yellow and green frames, and a black UW wayfinding pylon (yellow chevrons, "V1").
- **Roof terrace (`commons-v1-roof`, 2007):** square grey concrete pavers, raised concrete planter benches with cantilevered lips, a steel picket guard rail, and white globe lamps.
- **Approach:** the main walk runs off a drop-off loop and parking lot next to a lawn with lamp posts. Young red maples stand around the loop.
- **Missing:** each house's own elevation, the Great Hall and Mudie's exterior, the north and west outer faces, and aerial views.
