## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

Images in `private/web/` (git-ignored), sources in `private/web/web.jsonl`. Compass sides are inferred from context, not verified against the footprint; check against Street View/Earth captures.

4 photos (2013 Commons detail, 2019 news, uwaterloo aerial, c.2024 Stadium Journey). This folder is the largest CIF part. The Field House (2019) may be a separate catalog part (cif-37180-0 / cif-32194-0); check before using web_01.

- Main entrance (web_02 aerial, web_07): a circular asphalt drop-off with a planted island. The low entrance wing has dark low-slope canopy roofs on angled timber/steel brackets, a glazed storefront with doors, and Warriors graphics in the windows. Behind it are taller grey metal-clad gym/arena boxes with flat roofs and rooftop mechanical units.
- Walls: buff/tan brick with vertical reveals and a light metal fascia/clerestory (web_12).
- Field House (web_01, 2019): a tall box clad in brown ribbed metal, with a translucent green-tinted clerestory band along the top. A huge yellow panel carries a black Warrior-head logo and "UNIVERSITY OF WATERLOO" lettering. Stadium bleachers and the turf field sit beside it.
- Arena: interior photos show a barrel-vault glulam timber roof, but I excluded them. The exterior arena roof form is not seen.
- Missing: the arena exterior, the back and west sides, Columbia St frontage, the parking-lot sides and signage on the street side.
