# Waterloo College Hall

Status: not started

## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

This is a Wilfrid Laurier University first-year residence at 88 Seagram Drive, not a United College building. It opened in September 2002 with 312-318 singles, teaching space and the Laurier Bookstore. I found 1 image: a 2002 oblique aerial of the Laurier campus from wlu.ca, in which a site under construction with a crane (upper right) may be WCH. I have not verified this. The Laurier archive photos of WCH (images.ourontario.ca 2342192/2342189/2447540, shot from Seagram Drive) are behind a Cloudflare challenge, so I skipped them.
