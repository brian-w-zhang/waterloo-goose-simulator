# United College Green Wing

Status: not started

## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

2 images in `private/web/`. The Green Wing opened in 2017. Sources disagree on size: one says 8 storeys and 76 rooms, Wikipedia says 6 residence floors and 73 rooms. The ground floor holds Watson's Student Centre and the Student Commons.

- **01 (about 2017, elevated):** 8 storeys. One end is a dark brick pier/stair, and a full-height glazed stair runs at the corner. The main faces are a patchwork rainscreen of rectangular panels in white, light grey and dark grey, with scattered lime-green panels. Windows are regular and punched in a staggered rhythm. The ground floor is glazed. A landscaped path is in front, and the older brick building and the drum pavilion are at the right.
- **02 (2022):** The same panel patchwork and glazed stair, seen from the lawn by the wayfinding sign.
- **Missing:** the other sides, the roof, the entrance at grade and the main-building connection close up. Interiors (ribbon cutting, GreenHouse rendering) were removed.
