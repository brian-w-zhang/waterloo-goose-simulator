## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

Images in `private/web/` (git-ignored), sources in `private/web/web.jsonl`. Compass sides are inferred from context, not verified against the footprint; check against Street View/Earth captures.

7 photos, 1970s-2012 (one archive scan, Commons, Flickr 2008-2011). Nothing post-2018.

- Massing: low, stepped brutalist terraces of dark brown brick, each tier capped by a deep white fascia band. A tall brick chimney/stair tower rises above (web_01, web_08). The tiers step down a grassy slope toward University Ave (web_02 panorama, web_01).
- Materials: dark chocolate-brown brick, white (painted or precast) fascias, board-formed concrete retaining walls and stair cheeks (web_07). Glazing: the ground floor has a continuous storefront band under the white fascia (web_08). Upper tiers have tall, narrow window slots and recessed glazing.
- Entrances: one lower entrance is up a concrete stair between brick volumes, with a bronze plaque on the retaining wall (web_07). A 1970s archive scan shows an entrance with a "SOUTH CAMPUS HALL" sign on a concrete wall and a directional signpost (web_04). I can't say whether that sign survives.
- Surroundings: flagpoles (Canada, Ontario, UW) with a redbud/crabapple (web_05). There are open lawns toward DP Library and Graduate House to the north (web_03).
- Missing: all recent photos (post-2012), the Ring Road/basement entrance, the link to Hagey Hall, the W Store/Visitors Centre entrance and its signage, and roof/aerial. Window counts are not determinable.
