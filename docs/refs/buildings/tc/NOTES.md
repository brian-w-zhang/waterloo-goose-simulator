## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

Images in `private/web/` (git-ignored), sources in `private/web/web.jsonl`. Compass sides are inferred from context, not verified against the footprint; check against Street View/Earth captures.

16 photos, 2006-2020s (Commons 2013/2020, Flickr 2006-2010, uwaterloo.ca 2020s banners).

- Massing: long 3-storey bar (opened 2003). Two red-brown brick wings with a projecting full-height glass curtain-wall bay between them; this glass bay holds the main entrance (web_22, web_03). Further glass boxes sit at the ends and corners (web_04, web_07, web_20) and there is a glazed corner stair (web_18).
- Materials: dark red-brown brick (it reads more orange-red in sun) on the upper 2 floors. The ground floor is mostly continuous storefront glazing, with double-height glazing in places (web_21). Curtain walls have silver/grey aluminium mullions on an irregular grid with narrow vertical lites (web_10, web_20).
- Window rhythm: the brick bands have punched horizontal windows, singly or in pairs, irregularly spaced. There are roughly 6-8 per wing per floor on the long side (web_22, web_23). I can't give exact counts from these photos.
- Roof: flat. A light grey metal parapet/penthouse band with horizontal louvres runs along the top (web_18, web_23). A thin metal coping caps the brick.
- Entrances: the glazed main entry is in the central glass bay on the long facade, reached by a paved walk (web_03, web_22). A ground-floor entrance is recessed under the projecting glass upper floor, with a concrete column and bike racks (web_11).
- Signage and surroundings: a large black freestanding "UNIVERSITY OF WATERLOO" letter sign stands on a plaza in front, with yellow umbrellas and black pole lamps (web_24, 2020s). The "SOUTH CAMPUS / 200 University Avenue West" entry sign is on University Ave (web_08, 2008). The lawns have limestone block landscaping (web_23). A pale precast tower behind is probably Dana Porter Library (web_05, web_23).
- Missing: the back (ring-road/SCH side) elevation and the short ends in summer, close-ups of any building-name lettering on TC itself (none seen), and roof plan/aerial. Most photos show one long facade; I can't tell which compass side it is.
