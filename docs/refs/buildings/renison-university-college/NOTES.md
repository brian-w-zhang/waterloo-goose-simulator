# Renison University College

Status: not started

## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

8 images in `private/web/` (list and sources in `web.jsonl`). Dates: 01-04 are recent (2019-2024, from the uwaterloo.ca/Commons pages; 04 is dated 2020-06-01), 05 is about 2015, 06 is 2024, and 07-08 are from 2003 (before the 2005 Academic Centre). Compass directions are not confirmed from the photos. Check them against the ortho/Street View.

Background (from searches): the Founders Building dates from 1962 and the Luxton Building from 1964. The Chapel of St. Bede was added in 1992. The 2005 Academic Centre links the wings around a closed courtyard and holds the new main entrance and the Lusi Wong Library (east part). The English Language Institute building opened in 2015. Address: 240 Westmount Rd N, reached by a drive that runs down from Westmount.

- **Main entrance / parking-lot side (01, 04):** This is a large asphalt lot with a planted island. The main entrance is a tall pylon in tan/brown coursed stone veneer with vertical letters "R E N I S O N" and the crest near the top. Next to it is a glazed double-height lobby under a thin flat metal canopy that projects out on slim columns. The upper storey has a dark-grey metal fascia band. To the left, green-tinted curtain wall and buff block carry a small "Lusi Wong Library" sign. A long ramp with metal railings runs along the base, and a flagpole with four flags stands at the circle. In 01 the left block is buff/tan with a small RENISON sign high up. The centre block is the 3-storey Founders/academic wing: buff block, teal-framed windows, about 8+ bays visible. On the right is a 2-storey wing in random brown-and-tan stone veneer with a very low-pitched roof and an eave overhang (probably Luxton).
- **Lawn / path side (03, 02):** A secondary entrance has double glass doors at the top of concrete steps. "RENISON" letters sit on a white parapet band. The walls are buff split-face block with teal glass spandrel panels. Above the roofline you can see the folded zigzag clerestory (a row of glazed gable peaks). 02 shows a 2-storey wing behind cherry trees: brown stone ground floor, darker upper floor, and a low freestanding sign "RENISON UNIVERSITY COLLEGE / affiliated with the University of Waterloo" (white panel on a stone base).
- **Courtyard (06):** A timber footbridge sits in a planted courtyard. Behind it is a 3-storey wing with a zigzag roofline: about 6 glazed gable bays with white frames, stone veneer at the lower level.
- **Founders long elevation (08, from 2003):** A continuous zigzag glazed clerestory runs along the top. The ground floor is a colonnade of white round columns with red/maroon infill panels and a stone base. Bikes are parked under it. This elevation may have changed after 2005.
- **2015 ELI building (05):** Only one corner is visible behind people. It has vertical green-tinted glazed strips and white/light-grey metal panels.
