# CMH

Status: not started

## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

6 kept in `private/web/` (Masri O Architects photos 2016-17, UW pages, 2017 Daily Bulletin).

- **Massing:** a slab tower of about 13-14 storeys on a one-storey glazed podium (dining and commons). It stands beside the ION LRT corridor with the Beck/Eby towers to the north-west (`masrio-cmh-Ext-02`).
- **Materials:** red-brown brick with full-height vertical bands of buff/tan brick, and a recessed vertical strip of grey metal panel and glass at the stair. A darker brick base surrounds the entrance. The windows are punched, 2 per bay per floor, in a regular grid.
- **Podium:** floor-to-ceiling glazing with a thick white metal fascia band. On the courtyard side, grass amphitheatre terraces have concrete edges (`masrio-cmh-Ext-07`).
- **Entrance (`uw-cmh-hero`):** a white metal box canopy and concrete steps with a ramp and stainless rails. Coloured glass panels (blue, green, yellow, pink) line the ground floor, and a black/yellow CMH pylon stands by the door. A short drive and lay-by run in front.
- **Missing:** the back (south/east) faces, the roof, and a night view.
