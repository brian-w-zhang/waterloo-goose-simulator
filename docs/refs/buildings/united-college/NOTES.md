# United College

Status: not started

## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

7 images in `private/web/`. This college was called St. Paul's United College, then St. Paul's University College, and was renamed United College on 2022-09-24. Of the 7 images, 03 and 04 are recent (2022-23), 01 and 02 are undated, and 05-07 are from 2003. Address: 190 Westmount Rd N. The Green Wing (2017) and the Graduate Apartments (196 Westmount Rd N, 2004) have their own folders.

- **Main building long elevation (01):** 2-3 storeys in dark chocolate-brown brick with stepped, flat-roofed massing. The windows are small, irregular, white-framed punched openings, and there is one vertical slot window strip. "ST. PAUL'S UNITED COLLEGE" letters are at the corner (old signage). The access drive curves in front.
- **Entrance court (03, about 2023):** A single-storey drum pavilion has a precast concrete frieze with a geometric relief pattern and a shallow conical metal roof with a cylindrical vent/lantern. On either side are brown brick walls and a new glazed entrance. Above that entrance is a white panel with the blue "U" logo and "UNITED COLLEGE". There is a cyan-framed curtain wall on the left, room number 105 on the glass, and green Adirondack chairs on the patio.
- **Old entrance (05, 06, 2003):** Brown brick with raised white letters "ST. PAUL'S UNITED COLLEGE" over a glazed storefront between cream concrete piers, and steel railings. A white curved drum is visible at the right. This has probably been altered by the Green Wing and the rebrand.
- **Signage (02, 04):** 02 is the old hands-globe disc logo with "ST. PAUL'S UNIVERSITY COLLEGE" on brick (obsolete). 04 shows a current lawn wayfinding sign (white, blue U logo) reading "Grad Apartment Building / Lecture Hall UTD 105 / WISC / Access to Main Building".
- **Missing:** a recent view of the main building from Westmount, the chapel exterior, the east (campus) side, and roofs. I removed the interior of the chapel (bay window).
