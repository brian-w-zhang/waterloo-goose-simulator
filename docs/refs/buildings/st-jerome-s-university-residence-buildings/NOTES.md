# St. Jerome's University Residence Buildings

Status: not started

## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

4 images in `private/web/`: 2 real photos (2017 and recent), 1 rendering from 2015, and 1 distant winter view. Diamond Schmitt Architects designed the building and Graham built it in 2015-2017. It is a 7-storey, 360-bed residence in two wings, Siegfried Hall and Ryan Hall.

- **Front / drive side (01, 03):** Two slab wings meet at an obtuse angle with a recessed, fully glazed hinge that holds the stair/lobby (yellow interior glow). The cladding is dark red/maroon brick. Windows sit in continuous horizontal dark-grey (charcoal) bands, with alternating single windows and wider panels set in the bands. There are 7 floors above a fully glazed ground floor that is set back behind columns. "SIEGFRIED HALL" and "RYAN HALL" are in white letters on the fascia above each wing's ground-floor glazing. On the roof is a grey metal penthouse/mechanical volume with a curved (barrel) roof running along the slab. Next to it is a low dark-grey metal-clad building with a timber soffit (gym?). There is a drive loop with planted islands.
- **Rendering (02):** The Westmount-side elevation was shown with lighter vertical panels between the windows. The built building has darker charcoal bands, so do not trust the rendering for colour.
- **Distant (04):** A dark brick slab is seen across open ground and a road in winter, overcast.
- **Missing:** the back/courtyard elevations, the end walls, close-ups of the window module, and the roof.
