# Conrad Grebel University College

Status: not started

## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

17 images in `private/web/`. These include recent uwaterloo.ca photos, a Moriyama Teshima photo of the 2021 dining expansion, Grebel's then/now composite images (with inset circles), 2 magazine covers with text overlay, 1 rendering, archival B/W photos and 1 photo from 2003. Background: the residence and chapel date from 1963-64 (Barnett & Rieder-Hymmen) and the academic building from 1976 (Rieder-Hymmen). The residence was expanded in 2003 (atrium). The academic expansion was built in 2013-14 and the kitchen/dining expansion opened in 2021 (Moriyama Teshima). Address: 140 Westmount Rd N.

- **Front entrance / parking circle (05, 11, 13):** The 2013-14 academic expansion is 3 storeys with a curved corner. It is clad in white/silver metal panels with visible horizontal and vertical joints and has regular punched dark windows, about 10 bays on the long face. The ground floor is set back behind white round pillars and has a brick base with a glazed storefront. A low white sign reading "Conrad Grebel University College" stands at the drive. The expansion attaches to an older 3-storey block with vertical tan/beige and white stripes (piers with slot windows). The parking circle has planted islands and stairs.
- **1976 academic block (01, 02, 15):** A red-brown brick box, 3-4 storeys. The blank end wall carries "CONRAD GREBEL UNIVERSITY COLLEGE" in white serif letters near the top. A full-height glazed stair tower has white frames and smaller punched windows. It sits on a lawn bank with a path below. In 02 the United Green Wing is visible behind at the right.
- **Residence wings (04, 06, 09, 12, 17):** 3 storeys. The top floor is a zigzag row of folded glazed gables with white frames, which is the signature roofline. Below are alternating bands of tan/buff brick spandrels and white-framed window bands. The ground floor has a glazed lounge and patio. 12 shows a brick face with two tall white-framed glazed gable bays (the 2003 additions). In 06 the courtyard has a timber pergola and a green-glass atrium.
- **Chapel / dining hall (03, 07, 16):** A fieldstone (random rubble) building with a steep asymmetric gable roof in dark slate grey and a large band of windows below the eave. 07 (about 2021) shows the new dining pavilion, a long single-storey glazed volume with a dark metal fascia, with a roof terrace above it (pavers, tables, red planters). 14 shows the same expansion from the street: red brick base, a horizontal window band and a dark-grey metal upper band. 08 is the rendering of the expansion.
