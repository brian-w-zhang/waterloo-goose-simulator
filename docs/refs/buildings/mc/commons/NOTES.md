# MC (Mathematics & Computer): visual reference notes

Sources: 9 Commons photos, dated 2002 to 2013 (see `../ATTRIBUTION.csv`). No photo here is newer than 2013. M3 was built north of MC around 2018, and the MC/M3 glazed link is not shown in any of these photos. There is also no aerial or roof view.

Orientation used below comes from the OSM footprint in `data/raw/osm/map_11.osm`. MC spans about lat 43.4716 to 43.4725 and lon -80.5445 to -80.5434. SLC is directly west, QNC directly south (joined by a bridge at about 43.47166, -80.54397), C2 is east, and the MC Green lawn is to the north. Most uploaders do not say which side they photographed, so the side assignments below are inferences and are marked that way.

## Overall form (high confidence; consistent across all photos)
- The building is a brutalist block of warm light-grey precast and cast concrete. In midday light it reads as grey, roughly `#a7a3a2` to `#bebfb8`. Sunset photos (`MC_upper_floors_and_towers_sunset_2006.jpg`) make it look tan; that colour is not real.
- **Pilotis ground floor.** The ground floor is recessed behind big square concrete piers. The upper floors cantilever out over a covered walkway. Behind the piers the infill is dark-framed glazing, with panels of brown/red-brown brick (`MC_ground_floor_piers_detail_2013.jpg`).
- **Floor bands.** Above the recessed ground floor there is a thick concrete fascia band, then a lower ribbon of small square windows (about 1 m, close together, no hoods), then two upper storeys with hooded windows. Each hood is a projecting precast frame around a tall narrow window, and in raking light the hoods throw sawtooth shadows. A deep plain concrete parapet band runs above. In the main blocks the hooded windows form 2 continuous bands. Under each hood band there is a row of light trapezoid shapes, one per window (`MC_facade_across_green_summer_c2012.jpg`). I read these as the sloped undersides of the projecting hoods catching the light. They could also be separate angled sill brackets; this is uncertain.
- **Towers.** Tall, nearly windowless stair/service towers project from the facades and rise above the roofline. They come in clusters of 2 to 3 shafts of different heights and widths. Their concrete is **vertically fluted (finely ribbed)** (`MC_fluted_concrete_towers_detail_2013.jpg`, `MC_west_stair_tower_pink_tie_2002.jpg`). A narrow vertical slot of small windows runs in the recess between two shafts.
- **Height.** The towers reach about 7 storeys. The main blocks show roughly 4 above-ground storeys of facade over the recessed ground floor. OSM tags MC with `building:levels=7`.
- **Roof.** No photo shows it. Small rooftop penthouses and boxes are visible above the parapet in `MC_from_lawn_corner_two_facades_2004.jpg`, including a small antenna/mast on the central tower.

## Window rhythm / bay counts (approximate; counted off photos)
- `MC_from_lawn_corner_two_facades_2004.jpg`: the right-hand long block has about 30 to 32 hooded windows per band on its long face. The left block has about 17 to 18 per band in the visible part. The central tower cluster separates the two blocks.
- `MC_facade_across_green_summer_c2012.jpg`: about 26 hooded windows per band are visible before the tower at the right edge cuts the view. The window pitch is very regular, roughly 1.2 to 1.5 m centre to centre (estimated from people for scale).
- The ground-floor piers are much wider apart than the windows. Roughly 4 to 6 upper windows sit over each pier spacing, but this is uncertain.

## Per side
### West side (facing SLC): `MC_west_stair_tower_pink_tie_2002.jpg` (geotagged 43.47173, -80.54448)
- A 2-shaft fluted stair tower carries the famous giant pink tie banner, hung from the top of the taller shaft. Thin vertical slot windows sit in the recess between the shafts.
- To the right, the hooded-window block cantilevers over piers, and an exterior concrete stair with a dark railing climbs under the cantilever.
- At the tower base: brown brick infill, grass verge, and a black wayfinding post with white blade signs. One blade reads "Student Life Centre"; the other is unreadable.
- There is a small timber post or marker in a mulch bed with a few rocks, and a red-brown interlocking paver walkway.
- Photo date is **2002**, so the context may have changed. The pink tie is a recurring display, not a permanent fixture.

### Long facade facing a lawn (probably north/east toward MC Green and DC): `MC_facade_across_green_summer_c2012.jpg`, `MC_from_lawn_corner_two_facades_2004.jpg`
- A big mown lawn runs right up to the pier line. A worn dirt desire path crosses the lawn diagonally.
- Trees: a row of deciduous trees (some flowering horse-chestnut, some maples) and a tall blue/white spruce near the tower.
- Black slim lamp posts with small cap-type heads.
- In the 2004 image a sloped mulched bed with ornamental grasses sits at the left. A concrete walkway curves to the ground floor, a delivery truck is parked at the loading area, and people sit on the lawn.

### Facade facing QNC (south): `../QNC/QNC_MC_link_bridge_2011.jpg`
- This photo shows the top two MC hood bands over a plain concrete band.
- Below it, a long glazed bridge with horizontal aluminium louvres joins MC to the black ribbed-metal box of QNC.

### Distance views

The left of the frame shows an open-sided timber pergola/trellis structure and a mast sculpture or flagpole cluster near the brick buildings. A long 3-storey brown-brick building appears at right; I could not identify it.
- `MC_across_snowy_lawn_QNC_beyond_2013.jpg`: MC from a snowy lawn, with the QNC glass bar and its rooftop exhaust stacks beyond to the right.

## Entrances
- `MC_entrance_pier_chalk_tie_2007.jpg` (very likely MC; the uploader does not name the building) shows a typical ground-floor entrance:
  - double doors in black metal frames, with **warm-wood kick panels** across the lower third;
  - a flat black metal fascia canopy over the doors;
  - full-height black-framed glazing on either side;
  - a raw concrete pier in front with exposed-aggregate texture;
  - a light grey square concrete slab pavement.
- Which side this entrance is on is unknown. No photo shows the main entrance door count or its signage.

## Signage
- Only the wayfinding blade post near the west tower is visible (2002). No building-name sign appears in any photo.

## Missing / uncertain
- No confirmed full elevation of any single side. No clear south elevation except the bridge junction, and no clear east elevation (toward C2).
- No roof view and no photos after 2013. The M3 link and any changes after 2018 are unknown.
- Exact counts of storeys and windows per face are estimates.
