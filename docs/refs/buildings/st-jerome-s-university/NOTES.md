# St. Jerome's University

Status: not started

## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

5 images in `private/web/`. Most are old (2004-2005, before the 2015-2017 renewal) or show only parts of the campus. The new residence and SJ2 have their own folders.

Building names, from the SJU campus map at uwaterloo.ca/st-jeromes/location-and-maps (the map itself was not kept): 1 Academic Centre (SJ2); 2 Classrooms & Library Building (SJ1); 3 Douglas R. Letson Community Centre; 4 Health Services (UWaterloo); 5 J.R. Finn (JRF); 6 Louis Hall; 7 Notre Dame Chapel; 8 Ryan Hall and 9 Siegfried Hall (residences); 10 Sweeney Hall (former Notre Dame College); 11 The Funcken Cafe. The main entry and the residence entry come in from the Westmount Road side.

- **Core / quad (01):** A bronze statue on a granite plinth stands at a path junction. Behind it are low 1-2 storey buildings in brown/tan brick with shallow hipped dark roofs and wide eaves, plus a covered timber porch/pergola with lattice railings. There are mature trees and a red banner on a lamp post. The undated Commons photo is probably more than 10 years old.
- **J.R. Finn (02, 2005 night):** The gabled end of Finn has "J.R. FINN RESIDENCE" in raised letters and a lit stair window. In front is a flat-roofed glazed link/colonnade with square columns.
- **Sweeney Hall / walkway (04, recent banner):** This is a 2-storey brick wing (warm red-brown, white-framed 2-light windows) on the right. A glazed single-storey link runs on the left, with the 7-storey residence at the end of the walk.
- **Courtyard side (05, recent):** Brown/red brick with white-framed windows and a downpipe. Which wing this is has not been identified.
- **Parking (03, about 2004):** Only background stone and brick low-rises are visible. This photo is of limited use.
