## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

Images in `private/web/` (git-ignored), sources in `private/web/web.jsonl`. Compass sides are inferred from captions and context, not checked against the footprint; cross-check with Street View/Earth captures.

16 images (2009-2025). Hagey Hall is a 1970 brown-brick complex with two later additions: the 2009 School of Accounting and Finance wing (RHL Architects; northeast of the original) and the 2016 Hagey Hall Hub atrium over the old courtyard (MASRI O Architects).
- Original 1970 block / Humanities Theatre (web_01, 2025 Commons): large windowless brown-brick volumes stepping up to a tall fly-tower box with a darker metal-clad top; few small square windows; service/loading door and a driveway. Brick is uniform mid-brown, stretcher bond. web_06 brick close-up.
- SAF wing (web_02 entrance 2012, web_03 2009, web_20 2009 bulletin 'from the east'): three storeys, darker brown/grey brick with long horizontal ribbon windows, a glazed top storey with a thin projecting roof plane, a light grey cladding box frame over the entrance, glass canopy on steel posts at the door. "SCHOOL OF ACCOUNTANCY" type lettering is faintly visible on web_20 (not legible enough to quote).
- Hub (web_13/web_27/web_31 renderings, web_14 dusk 2016, web_21 2016, web_23, web_24 construction 2016): a glazed atrium with a tall glass stair/elevator tower carrying large vertical letters "ARTS" (A, R, T, S stacked, white frit) facing the paved courtyard/plaza; flat projecting roof caps; brick wings of the original building either side. Paved plaza with benches and lamp posts in front.
- web_12: tan/bronze perforated metal panel wall with a door (location in HH uncertain, possibly the theatre volume). web_05: green roof garden (2010). web_28/web_35: courtyard at night before the Hub (2014-15, forum).
