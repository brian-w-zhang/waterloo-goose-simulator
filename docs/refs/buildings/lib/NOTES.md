## Web photos (agent, 2026-11-03)

Images in `private/web/` (git-ignored), sources in `private/web/web.jsonl`. Compass sides are inferred from captions and context, not checked against the footprint; cross-check with Street View/Earth captures.

25 images (2006-2025; recent: web_01 2025, web_02 2020, web_11 2023 front render/drawing, web_13/web_20 2017-2020). Best side set is the library's own Flickr account, 2010-10-15 (web_14 west side, web_15 east-side staircase, web_16 north side, web_17 main entrance, web_18 accessibility ramp).
- Massing: a near-cubic tower (about 10 storeys) sits on a wider, lower podium. The podium overhangs a recessed ground floor carried on splayed, fan-shaped concrete piers. Between the piers, round-arched glazed bays (5 arches across one face in web_11). The podium/tower junction is a dark recessed band.
- Tower skin: cream/light-grey precast concrete with closely spaced vertical fins/ribs and narrow vertical slot windows between them. Very regular rhythm, windows stacked in floor-height strips (web_11 drawing shows about 22 slot columns across one face; count approximate). Lit at night it reads as a grid of small bright rectangles (web_03, web_23, web_28).
- Podium fascia: white precast band with close vertical mullions over a dark band, above the arches.
- Base: rough-faced grey ashlar/split-face stone plinth wall below the arcade, with a continuous light-grey steel guardrail along the arcade terrace (web_14, web_16). Broad concrete stairs with steel handrails up to the main entrance; a long switchback ramp beside it (web_17, web_18). Doors are aluminium/glass inside the arches.
- Roof: flat; a thin antenna/mast near the centre (web_01, web_03, web_20).
- Surroundings: open lawn and plaza toward Hagey Hall and ML; red sculptures in the plaza (web_04, 2009); a snowy patio with tables at the podium base (web_06).
- Uncertain: web_12 is a close-up of a ribbed grid facade captioned only "Ring Road" (probably LIB, not certain). web_22 is a B&W film shot of the arcade.
- Missing: true aerial/roof view, clear straight-on elevation of each tower face with a counted window grid, signage (no building-name sign seen), loading/service side.
