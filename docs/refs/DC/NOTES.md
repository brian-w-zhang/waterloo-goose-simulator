# DC (William G. Davis Computer Research Centre): visual reference notes

Sources: 8 Commons photos, dated 2006 to 2025 (see `../ATTRIBUTION.csv`). The newest is `DC_entrance_plaza_east_2025.jpg` (June 2025), and it matches the 2010 to 2016 photos closely, so the exterior seems stable. There are no aerial views.

Orientation: DC is not named in the local OSM extract. Campus maps place it north-east of MC and C2, with Ring Road and then the rail/ION corridor on its east side (toward E5). `DC_east_facade_from_rail_corridor_2014.jpg` was taken from across the railway tracks, so that long side faces east. Where other photos were taken from is inferred.

## Overall form (high confidence)
- **The main volume is a long barrel vault of glass.** It is a curtain wall that runs up the wall and curves over into the roof. The glass is green/teal tinted, the mullions silver/grey, and the grid is regular with small panes.
  - Mullion bays: roughly 18 to 22 vertical bays between successive duct elbows (counted on `DC_east_facade_from_rail_corridor_2014.jpg`).
  - Pane rows: about 13 to 15 from the podium to the crown (`DC_entrance_red_frames_closeup_2010.jpg`).
  - Some panes are opaque grey/brown spandrels in irregular vertical stripes, matching floor slabs and stairs behind.
  - Through the glass you can see yellow-painted stairs and catwalks and red structure (`DC_entrance_and_low_wing_2012.jpg`, `DC_lit_bridges_night_2009.jpg`).
- **Duct elbows.** Large teal-grey (`~#4e6f72`) round ducts rise vertically against the vault face, then bend over the curved roof like inverted-J "candy canes". Each terminates in a horizontal ribbed metal housing along the roof ridge.
  - 3 elbows are visible along the long east side in 2014 and about 4 in the 2012 view, spaced about 25 to 30 m apart (estimate).
  - Each elbow has a red collar ring about two-thirds of the way up (`DC_entrance_red_frames_closeup_2010.jpg`).
- **The low wings are 2 to 3 storeys** and clad in tan/beige metal or precast panels with **horizontal silver ribbed banding**, sometimes reading as corrugated stripes, and rounded corners.
  - Windows are horizontal ribbon strips and square punched windows.
  - The ground floor has large glazed openings, some with orange/red interior visible.
  - The wings step back in tiers (`DC_entrance_plaza_east_2025.jpg` shows 3 stepped levels at left).
- **Red steel ("high-tech") entrance structures**, glossy red `~#c8202a`:
  - Freestanding tall round **masts** with short horizontal outriggers carrying lamp heads.
  - A flat **canopy** over the entrance doors, held by diagonal tie rods from the masts.
  - Large **portal frames** of 2 to 3 big round red columns supporting deep red I-beam lintels, sometimes with circular holes. The portals stand in front of a **pale silver-white corrugated or fluted metal-clad box**, one storey high with a green-edged parapet. The box is blank apart from a small door.
- **The glazed bridge** is two storeys of glazing on red steel posts. It spans a service road and links DC with the building on the other side (`DC_glazed_bridge_and_vault_from_E5_2012.jpg`). There are X-braces in the glazing. The far building may be E3/E2; I did not confirm this.
- **Elevated glazed section on red columns** (`DC_lit_bridges_night_2009.jpg`): two floors of glazing with dark spandrel bands are lifted over an open walkway on slender red columns, with bike racks underneath. Which side this is on is not known.

## Per side / entrance
### East side (rail/Ring Road side): `DC_east_facade_from_rail_corridor_2014.jpg`, `DC_from_ring_road_walkway_2006.jpg`
- From left to right: the tan banded low wing with ribbon glazing; the main entrance (red masts, tie rods, red canopy, glass doors under the vault); then the pale metal box with **two red portal frames** standing in front of it; then a tan building edge at far right.
- Ground plane in front:
  - a concrete walkway along the building with long concrete bench blocks;
  - grey/silver street lamps with a curved arm and an elliptical head;
  - small spruces and pines, and bare deciduous trees;
  - a wide concrete walkway, and a mown or dry-grass verge along the tracks;
  - many bikes parked along the wall.
- 2006: purple-leaf ornamental trees (probably Crimson King maple or purple-leaf plum) in front, and a red-brown interlocking paver walk (`DC_from_ring_road_walkway_2006.jpg`).

### Entrance plaza (probably the same east entrance, later state): `DC_entrance_plaza_east_2025.jpg`, `DC_entrance_red_frames_closeup_2010.jpg`
- The plaza is large and paved with **grey interlocking concrete pavers** in a herringbone or running bond. They look grey, with brown hints.
- Square tapered **exposed-aggregate concrete planters** hold canna/tropical plantings (2025).
- Galvanised steel **inverted-U bike racks** sit on a rail, and more bike racks run along the low wing. A small orange/red sculptural bike rack or bench stands near the doors.
- A 3-bin black waste/recycling station with coloured labels stands by the entrance, along with an A-frame sign.
- At the plaza edge: a tall black lamp post with a flat round head, a Lombardy-poplar-like columnar tree row, and lawn.

### Second entrance (mirror arrangement): `DC_entrance_and_low_wing_2012.jpg`
- The red portal columns are at the left and the canopy entrance is in the centre. The tan two-level low wing with curved corners and silver bands is at right.
- Red "wave" bike racks stand on the lawn edge.
- This is probably a different face from the east entrance because the arrangement is reversed. Its compass side is unconfirmed.

### Paved approach: `DC_paver_walkway_approach_panorama_2016.jpg`
- A straight walkway of rectangular grey-brown concrete pavers in a basket/running pattern leads to the pale metal box framed by a long **red portal beam**, with the vault rising behind.
- Left: a 3 to 4 storey building with tan horizontal banded cladding and a full glass ground floor. It may be a DC wing or EIT/C2; this is uncertain.
- Along it: grey slatted benches, a picnic table, trees in round mulch rings, and a lawn.
- Right: a dense group of pines and spruces. In the distance, a tan precast tower (probably MC or C2).
- April 2016, light snow.

## Roof
- The vault crown is continuous glazing. Ribbed metal duct housings run along the ridge. The flat roofs of the low wings carry plant equipment (`DC_glazed_bridge_and_vault_from_E5_2012.jpg`).
