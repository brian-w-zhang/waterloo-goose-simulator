# QNC (Mike & Ophelia Lazaridis Quantum-Nano Centre): visual reference notes

Sources: 10 Commons photos (see `../ATTRIBUTION.csv`).
- 2 are from construction, Oct 2010 (`QNC_under_construction_*`).
- 2 are from spring 2012, just before completion (`QNC_bar_and_cube_upward_2012.jpg`, `QNC_honeycomb_cube_through_trees_2012.jpg`).
- The rest show the finished building in 2011 to 2013.
- The infrared image has a wrong camera date (2005). The building was not built then; treat it as about 2012.
- There are no photos after 2013.

Orientation from OSM: the footprint spans about lat 43.4709 to 43.4716 and lon -80.5449 to -80.5435. That puts QNC directly south of MC and south-east of SLC, with Ring Road along its south side. The honeycomb cube is at the **east** end: `QNC_honeycomb_cube_east_face_from_plaza_2011.jpg` is captioned "facing west". The long finned glass bar is at the **west** end, toward SLC.

## Massing (high confidence)
1. **Podium.** A 1 to 2 storey base clad in **charcoal/dark grey brick** of mixed tones. Lighter grey bricks are scattered in a horizontal dashed pattern (`QNC_bar_and_cube_upward_2012.jpg`).
   - Above the brick sits a **band of silver horizontal metal louvres** about one storey high, which hides the mechanical/clean-room floors.
   - The podium roof is a **green roof** with grasses and shrubs (`QNC_south_facade_from_ring_road_2013.jpg`).
2. **The bar (west/lab-office block)** is 4 glazed storeys, cantilevered well out over a recessed, glazed ground floor.
   - Its curtain wall has **strong vertical fins/stripes alternating silver-reflective and dark grey glass**. There are about 16 to 18 stripe pairs on the south face (counted on `QNC_south_facade_from_ring_road_2013.jpg`); the count is approximate.
   - Small clear vision windows are set into the stripes at irregular heights.
   - The soffit under the cantilever is dark, and there is a slim parapet line.
   - A flat roof carries a few small vents and davit arms (window-cleaning cranes).
3. **The cube (east/quantum block)** is a near-cube of silver-blue reflective glass, about 6 to 7 storeys, with an **external hexagonal "honeycomb" frame** of light-grey/silver steel members.
   - The hexagons are tall (each spans about 2 storeys) and arranged in staggered columns. Each cube face shows about 3 full hexagon columns plus half-hexes at the edges, in about 3.5 rows.
   - The frame stands off the glass on short brackets, with clamp nodes at the vertices.
   - Behind the frame are small rectangular vision windows in pairs (one wide, one narrow).
   - Two window-cleaning davit arms sit on the roof (2011 photo). Rooftop exhaust stacks (3 tall silver stacks plus smaller ones) are seen from E5 and SLC (`../DC/DC_glazed_bridge_and_vault_from_E5_2012.jpg`, `../SLC/SLC_east_pergola_walkway_QNC_beyond_2020.jpg`).
4. **The link box** is a tall **black horizontally ribbed metal** volume between QNC and MC. A glazed bridge with horizontal louvres joins it to MC's upper floor (`QNC_MC_link_bridge_2011.jpg`). A round concrete column supports the bridge.

## Per side
### South (Ring Road): `QNC_south_facade_from_ring_road_2013.jpg`, `QNC_south_facade_dusk_2012.jpg`
- Left to right: the finned bar cantilevering over a glazed ground floor; the podium brick wall with its louvre band above; a recessed **orange/red glazed panel** about 3 to 4 panes wide in the podium (lit vivid orange at dusk); then the cube rising behind the podium on the east.
- At dusk the whole ground-floor glazing under the bar glows orange-red (2012).
- Street level:
  - a concrete sidewalk with a planted strip of shrubs and young trees;
  - **two low monument signs**: a grey/white panel on a concrete plinth reading "Student Life Centre" with a directory list, and a smaller white panel reading "Mike & Ophelia Lazaridis Quantum-Nano Centre";
  - a black lamp post with a round cap head;
  - traffic barrels (2013, temporary);
  - at the east end, a service driveway and bollards.
- The road is two-lane asphalt with a yellow centre line and a white-striped parking/bus lane on one side.

### East (plaza / rock garden side): `QNC_honeycomb_cube_east_face_from_plaza_2011.jpg`, `QNC_honeycomb_cube_through_trees_2012.jpg`
- The full cube face is visible: 3 hex columns, with the ground floor recessed and glazed in dark frames below.
- At left there is a glass stair/circulation slot.
- Foreground:
  - a plaza of **small square grey/buff concrete pavers**;
  - a low round concrete planter curb;
  - large limestone and granite boulders on mulch (the rock garden);
  - a black lamp post with a small conical/cap head;
  - a **round poster kiosk** with a conical cap and a circular timber bench;
  - a square concrete trash bin;
  - maples (orange in October) and pines.

### North (MC side): `QNC_MC_link_bridge_2011.jpg`
- Only the ribbed black link box and the louvred bridge are documented.

### West (SLC side)
- Only seen at an angle, in `../SLC/SLC_east_pergola_walkway_QNC_beyond_2020.jpg` (the bar's west part, with stacks on the roof). No dedicated photo.

## Construction-era photos (2010)
