NE corner (east of Phillip): Campus Court, 150 University Ave W = buildings/oid31641. It is a buff-brick L/U with a central block, sharing one lot with the 140 University Ave W strip. That strip has Mel's Diner, Onnuri & Pub, momo, Waterloo Central Supermarket, mochi mochi, UniClaw and bb.q Village; its catalog footprint is merged into buildings/waterloo-central-supermarket (fork A's folder), so its photos are kept here.
South side of University (SE/SW corners) is UW Place / CMH (residences), not retail.
Further east: 203 Lester (buildings/203-lester-street) has a glass retail podium at University and Lester. 280 Lester (Sage 5, buildings/sage-5) has ground-floor retail along Lester and Hickory.

## Look and materials

- **Plaza west of Phillip.**
  - Walls and fascia: one storey, about 4.5-5 m to the eave. Continuous brown/dark-brown asphalt-shingle mansard roof band, with flat roof and rooftop exhaust stacks behind. Fascia is cream horizontal vinyl siding with individual tenant box signs or channel letters. Short square columns carry a narrow walkway canopy (tan stucco on the 160 strip in 2006, brick piers in places).
  - Glazing: full aluminium storefronts, heavily covered in food-photo decals.
  - Gables: the NW corner block (Pho Anh Vu) and the turn of the 160 strip have taller gables, one an angular tan A-frame.
  - Lot and signage: one large cracked asphalt lot with yellow stall lines and tall single-head light poles. The pylon on a landscaped island at the University Ave entrance is dark grey, with a Williams Fresh Cafe top panel and red tenant panels below. A white "UNIVERSITY SHOPS PLAZA ONLY" parking sign stands at the lot edge. Landscape strips use river stone.
  - Skyline: E7's white faceted panels rise directly behind the north row. Three beige 18-20 storey towers (Phillip/Lester) sit behind the 160 strip to the NE.
- **Campus Court, 150.**
  - Walls and roof: buff/yellow brick, brown shingle hip roof, brick piers with a covered walkway, white box sign bands, red-framed doors.
  - Tower: a buff brick tower with a pyramid roof stands at the corner.
- **140 strip.**
  - Walls and roof: buff brick piers, white vinyl front gables (Onnuri), dark shingle roofs.
  - Lot: shares the lot with Campus Court. The Lester St towers are behind.
- **Sage 5 retail (280 Lester).**
  - Podium: light grey composite panels and aluminium glazing, glass canopy, black box signs.
- **203 Lester podium.**
  - Podium: 2-3 storey glass curtain wall with a glazed gable over the entry and white metal sunshade fins, on a paver forecourt with broad steps.
