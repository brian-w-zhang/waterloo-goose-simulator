# Rez-One (Phillip Square towers)

Rez-One (rez-one.ca) is the operator brand for Blair House (256 Phillip) and Elora House (250 Phillip); Fergus (254) and Hespeler (252) are Forum Asset Management buildings in the same complex, built 2015-2022 (Ontario Community Newspapers note: Elora was the 4th and last building of "Phillip Square", ~1,800 students). Per-building photos are in docs/refs/buildings/{blair,elora,fergus,hespeler}-house; this folder holds complex-wide shots.
