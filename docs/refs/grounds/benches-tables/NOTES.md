# Benches and picnic tables

- OSM inside the boundary (`private/data/osm_in_boundary.geojson`): **111 benches and 20 picnic tables**.
  - backrest: yes 51 / no 31.
  - material: wood 31, steel 5, concrete 3, stone 1.
  - colour: brown 8, red 6, maroon 6, grey 3.
- There is a memorial bench for C.J. Moore near E2 / Ring Road (-80.54045, 43.47051).
- No UW furniture inventory or standard is published.

## Bench and table types seen
- **Landscape Forms MultipliCITY (newer, "South Common" project):**
  - Source: https://www.landscapeforms.com/ideas/univsersity-of-waterloo. Landscape architect Mark Zuzinjak; date not stated.
  - Products: **MultipliCITY tables and benches**, backless and backed benches, **Legrand outdoor charging stations** (slim grey square posts about 1.1 m), **TUUCI sun shades** (yellow parasols), and **Ring bike racks with repair stations**.
  - Photos (`private/web/000-007`):
    - **Tables:** wide warm-brown **wood-slat tops** on **bright yellow** powder-coated steel C-frame legs, with integrated benches, so they work as picnic tables. A table is about 2.4 m long.
    - **Backless benches:** wood slats on yellow or silver frames, and very long (about 6 m) benches on silver frames along planting beds.
    - **Ground:** light grey concrete paving with grey unit-paver bands; young trees and planting beds.
    - **Yellow market umbrellas** over tables near a big lawn.
  - **Location:** unconfirmed; the vendor calls it "South Common". From the photos it appears to be in south campus. One image shows a white precast multi-storey façade like Dana Porter / ML in the background. Check against `docs/refs/zones`.
  - I did not look up product dimensions. Get them from the Landscape Forms MultipliCITY spec sheets if needed; standard seat height is about 0.45 m.
- **Older timber benches:** timber slats on **concrete feet or legs** (SLC pergola, rock garden), and heavy **timber block benches** (rock garden pergola).
  - The rock garden map shows about 14 benches (`../peter-russell-rock-garden/`).
- **Maroon / red metal picnic tables** (SLC 2007; OSM colour red / maroon), later replaced in places by timber-topped tables.
- **Grey slatted metal benches** (DC walkway, 2016).
- **Concrete bench blocks** (DC east) and **concrete seat walls** (Arts Quad 2018 redesign; see `../arts-quad/`).
- **Arts Quad (2018, Bennett Oh / GSP Group):** ArchDaily lists the manufacturers as **Hauser Site Furniture**, **Cornilleau** (outdoor ping-pong tables) and **LollTrade** (recycled-plastic furniture, for example Adirondack chairs).
- Circular timber benches around poster kiosks (MC / QNC plaza; see `../grounds-commons/NOTES.md`).
