# Wayfinding, monument and Ring Road signs

## The 2017-2023 Campus Wayfinding Project
Sources: https://uwaterloo.ca/wayfinding/ and /project-updates.
- The project was launched in 2017. Plant Operations coordinated it, with a steering committee and an advisory group. No design firm is named.
- **Exterior roll-out:**
  - Building signs: first wave February 2020.
  - Sign bases: from March 2020.
  - Pedestrian, parking and Campus Housing signs: from April 2020.
  - Signs for buildings, paths, maps, parking and roads: by July 2020.
  - Entrance-way signs: late summer 2020.
  - Old signs were removed or relocated from August 2020.
  - **All exterior signage was complete as of March 2023.** Phase 4 (enhancements) is approved.
- A 2024 tender, "CGB Replace YAH wayfinding signage", replaced you-are-here map signs.
- **Sign families** (from the project text): building ID, pedestrian directional, campus maps (you-are-here), parking-lot, road / vehicular, campus entrance and Campus Housing.
- **Appearance of the new family (from photos):**
  - **Black / very dark charcoal panels with white sans-serif text** (UW brand typeface; probably the brand's sans, unverified).
  - Tall slim **totems** about 2.2-2.5 m high and 0.5-0.6 m wide at path junctions (`../ring-road/private/web/003_ring-road.jpg`).
  - Building ID signs are low black blades or monoliths at entrances with the building code and name. Dimensions unknown.
  - **Verify against Street View** (`docs/refs/streetview/`). No published size spec was found. Brand wayfinding assets: https://uwaterloo.ca/brand/downloads-and-resources/wayfinding-and-digital-signage-assets.

## Road signs and pedestrian crossovers
- In-road **"STOP FOR PEDESTRIANS"** flexible signs at Ring Road crossovers (fluorescent yellow-green). See `../ring-road/NOTES.md`.
- Standard Ontario regulatory signs (STOP, YIELD, and so on) at internal junctions. OSM has 50 stop and 8 give-way nodes inside the boundary.
- City `SignInventory` (street signs with SIGN_HEIGHT / SIGN_WIDTH) has **no records in our bbox**.

## OSM
`private/data/osm_in_boundary.geojson` includes 11 `tourism=information` nodes, for example "Waterloo Visitors Desk", "Peter Russell Rock Garden" and "Disc Golf Course" boards.
