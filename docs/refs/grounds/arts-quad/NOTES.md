# Arts Quad / south campus quad (Dana Porter, ML, Hagey Hall, PAS, EV area)

## The 2018 Arts Quad outdoor spaces (Bennett Oh, with GSP Group)
Source: ArchDaily, "Waterloo Arts Quad Outdoor Spaces / Bennett Oh" (2019-10-04). 30 images are in `private/web/001-032`.
- **Completed 2018** for "Celebration 2017" (UW's 60th and Canada's 150th). The area is **1860 m²**.
  - Lead architect Reema Masri; landscape by GSP Group; built with UW Plant Operations.
  - Furniture makers listed: **Hauser Site Furniture**, **Cornilleau** (ping-pong tables), **LollTrade**.
- **Theme: a computer circuit board ("CPU").**
  - **Yellow painted circuit traces** on the grey asphalt and concrete: lines ending in **yellow ring "solder pads"** about 0.5 m diameter, in arrays.
  - A pale **green "chip" rectangle** is painted on the paving.
- **Plan** (`001_20171028_layout_page.jpg`, scale bar 10 m):
  - **"CPU":** a central raised square platform about **13 x 14 m** with notched (crenellated) edges, square corner blocks, and steps on the north and south faces.
  - **Stepped wavy terraces:** 3-4 concrete seat tiers in an S-curve on either side of the CPU (north-to-south "river" of steps). They have timber-topped seats and yellow edge lines.
  - **A large circular raised tree planter** of about **14 m diameter** around a mature tree (SW), with chairs around its rim.
  - 2 **Cornilleau outdoor ping-pong tables** (blue tops, concrete-grey bodies).
  - A "bar" counter with stools (west).
  - "Mingling" clusters: square and cross-shaped modular seating blocks around small trees in black square planters, plus **dark green / black Adirondack chairs** (LollTrade recycled plastic).
  - "Focus" tables for 2-4 people; "Lounging" round stools.
  - Bike parking rows on the north and south edges.
  - Vehicle parking or a service lane on the west side.
- **Planting:**
  - Linear beds of **ornamental grasses** (feather reed grass type) in mulch along concrete curbs.
  - Large timber-clad or Corten square planters with grasses and young trees.
  - Mature deciduous trees (maples, honey locust) and existing spruces at the edges.
- **Edges** (from photos):
  - **ML (Modern Languages):** dark brown brick, tall narrow windows, an exterior stair with a galvanised railing, a timber bench row on a concrete pad.
  - **Hagey Hall / the Humanities Theatre** (white).
  - **PAS** (brown brick).
  - **Dana Porter Library:** a white 10-storey tower with precast panels on a dark recessed base; the campus landmark visible from across the city.
  - **EV3** (white and black, glass).
- Lamps: tall black poles with small LED heads.
