# Village Green and the north campus fields

## Facts
- **"Village Green"** is a label on the official campus maps, on the north side near **Field 3 and Field 7**, close to the Columbia Icefield (CIF) and Village 1 / REV. In practice it is the large open lawn and fields between the residences (V1, REV, MKV) and the CIF complex. No source describes its features beyond the label.
- **Columbia Icefield complex** (north campus): "five playing fields, three gyms, an arena and a baseball diamond".
  - **Warrior Field:** synthetic turf, lit. It was upgraded in 2009-2010 to **FieldTurf Duraspine PRO** and has a newer turf (Imprint).
    - Used for football, field hockey, soccer and flag football. Photospheres at about (-80.5497, 43.4744).
    - It has stands and lighting masts (StadiumJourney photos `004-013`).
  - **CIF Field House** (opened about 2018, 65,000 sq ft, indoor turf) sits **just north of Warrior Field**.
  - A "Playing fields" photosphere point is at (-80.55335, 43.47513).
- **EcoMap Landscaped_Areas:** 13 "Field" polygons, mown by Plant Ops. Soil pH and fertiliser research is done on the playing fields.
- **Brubacher House** (1850s Pennsylvania-German Mennonite stone farmhouse, now a museum) with its **grounds**: lawns, a heritage garden and old trees. It sits between the playing fields and Columbia Lake. See `013_*`, `014_*` (UW Brubacher House "Grounds" page). Lot "Brubacher House": 18 stalls.
- **Residence quads:**
  - **Village 1** (1960s; low brick blocks around courtyards; the photosphere at -80.55057, 43.47148).
  - **REV** (Ron Eydt Village) with the "REV north quad".
  - **MKV** (Mackenzie King Village; Commons photo and sod-turning).
  - Green triangular bike racks (see `../bike-racks/`).
  - The **REV forest** (mostly conifers) lies between REV and Westmount Rd.
- **Fire pits:** CL1 and CL2 on the playing fields by Columbia Lake (UW open data).
