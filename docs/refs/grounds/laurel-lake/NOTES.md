# Laurel Lake

**Correction to the brief:** Laurel Lake is on **south campus**, not north campus. Columbia Lake is the north-campus lake.

## Facts
- **Position:** OSM "Laurel Lake" centre about (-80.54402, 43.46688), extent about 108 x 177 m. It is near the University Ave entrance, between Ring Road (south-west part), St Paul's / St Jerome's and the colleges bridge, and EV / PAS.
  - City layer "Laurel Lake", Lake, **6,610 m²**. The City polygon is smaller than OSM's extent, possibly after the 2004 reshaping.
  - A small storm-water management pond is just south at (-80.54371, 43.46548), 714 m² (City).
- **History** (Bulletin 1999, 2004):
  - A man-made reservoir on Laurel Creek, one of two lakes on the creek on south campus. The other is the Health Services pond; see `../strauss-lake/`.
  - Its water level was controlled by a dam at Columbia St (per the 2004 Bulletin; probably the upstream dam that controls flow).
  - In **2004** bank stabilisation began, aiming for a **smaller, deeper lake with faster flow**.
- **Laurel Creek Fire Pit:** "East-side of Laurel Lake along the Ring Road" (UW open data `firepits`).
- **Wildlife:** great blue herons and mallards (Flickr, `private/web/000_*`). Geese nest here.
- **Look:** a narrow, naturalised pond with a shrubby and treed shoreline (willows, dogwood, cattails). The water is brown-green. Footbridges and asphalt paths along the creek connect to the colleges ("Bridge to University Colleges" photosphere at -80.5443, 43.4683).
