# Columbia Lake (north campus, Environmental Reserve)

## Facts
- **Position:** OSM multipolygon centre about (-80.55718, 43.47353). Its extent is about **466 m E-W by 534 m N-S**.
  - City `WaterFeatures_WaterBodies`: "Columbia Lake", Lake, **AREA 114,754 m² (about 11.5 ha)**. EcoMap Waterbodies lists a lake of 10.8 ha.
  - Polygons: `../maps/private/data/city_waterbodies_bbox.geojson` and `../maps/private/data/uw_waterbodies.geojson`.
- **Origin:** a man-made **reservoir on Laurel Creek**, built for aesthetics more than flood control, like Laurel Lake.
  - Laurel Creek flows through it and **warms** as it does (student report), which hurts creek water quality (1999 Bulletin).
  - In **2005** it was **dredged and deepened** as part of the UW **Environmental Reserve** (109 ha along the Laurel Creek corridor). A bypass channel to route the creek around the lake was planned but postponed (Bulletin 2005-08-12).
  - Geese wade on the mudflats.
- **Surroundings:**
  - North campus **playing fields** (Columbia Icefield fields, Warrior Field) to the east and north-east.
  - **Columbia Lake Village** (student and family townhouses) and its forest to the west and south-west.
  - **Brubacher House** (1850s Mennonite farmhouse museum) between the lake and the fields.
  - A **disc golf course** around the lake (OSM info board).
  - **Fire pits:** CL1 on the east side of the lake on the playing fields; CL2 between the lake and Brubacher House (UW open data).
  - Trails: the "University of Waterloo Trail" (City trail layer, asphalt / stonedust).
- **Look in summer:**
  - Open, shallow lake with grassy and cattail edges, scattered willows and shrubs.
  - A mown trail ring with benches.
  - **Columbia Lake sign:** a timber or stone entrance sign (Commons `000_Columbia_Lake_sign.jpg`).
  - Large goose flocks.
  - The north end has a weir / dam structure where Laurel Creek leaves the lake. Not photographed here.
