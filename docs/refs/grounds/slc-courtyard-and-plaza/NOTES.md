# SLC courtyard, plaza and Great Lawn

## Facts
- **SLC / PAC expansion:** construction started May 2017, and the SLC courtyard was closed during the work. The **grand opening of the expanded SLC and PAC was in 2022**. Architects: CS&P.
  - The addition frames an outdoor social space the architects call the **"Great Lawn"**.
  - Photos 004-011 (Flickr, 2022, winter) show the new façade: a two-storey **pale green-tinted curtain wall** with vertical fins over a glazed ground floor on slim grey columns, and a tall dark-grey stair / entrance tower.
- **SLC Dining Hall addition** (ATRR Architects, project in progress, renders `000`, `001`): an eastward extension with an outdoor patio. The render shows a white perforated-metal façade.
- **Indigenous outdoor gathering space**, *Skén:nen Tsi Nón:we Tewaya'taróroks*:
  - On the **green between SLC and Math 3 (M3)**.
  - Construction began 2023 or 2024; it was named on 30 September 2024.
  - It is a **timber pavilion / arbour** of round log columns and a shallow layered roof, around a gathering circle, with interpretive signage and native plantings. It is used for teaching (UW News 2024; `002_*`).
- **Older SLC courtyard** (from photos about 2007-2016, per `../grounds-commons/NOTES.md`):
  - Light grey square pavers with charcoal paver bands.
  - A round raised concrete planter with a tree.
  - Timber-slat benches on concrete feet under a pergola.
  - Maroon metal picnic tables.
  - A Commons photo shows a **SLC patio** (Bombshelter pub patio).
- The Strauss Lake / Health Services pond and Alumni Lane "Class of" trees are just west and north-west (see `../strauss-lake/`, `../trees/`).
