# Seasons: what campus looks like (target: late spring / summer)

## Recommended target: late May to July
- **Late May / June:**
  - Full fresh-green deciduous canopy; maples and lindens are fully leafed by late May.
  - **Lawns lush green and mowed weekly.** Plant Ops mows lawns; fields are fertilised (EcoMap).
  - Dandelions dot the lawns in mid-to-late May (Flickr "Goose & Dandelions").
  - Crabapple and serviceberry blossom happens in mid-May, about 1-2 weeks; lilacs in late May.
  - **Goslings** are present. Beds are planted with annuals.
  - Long days: sunset about 21:00 EDT near the solstice.
- **July / August:**
  - Darker green canopy. Lawns can go **patchy and straw-brown in dry spells**; unirrigated lawns browned noticeably in the 2021 summer videos (unverified for any given year).
  - Tall meadow grass and goldenrod in the Environmental Reserve and on the naturalised Laurel Creek banks.
  - Columbia Lake gets algae and duckweed (shallow lake; dredged in 2005).
- Late-spring / summer video and photo references:
  - `../walking-tours/`: 90kQJHtJmH4 (2026-05-30) and NHgM9-Ic0NE (June 2021).
  - Commons "BMH Greens", "South Campus Greens", the MC Green panorama.
  - Flickr "Summer - University of Waterloo".

## Other seasons, for contrast
- **Autumn** (late September to October): maples go red and orange, honey locusts yellow. Many references here: Commons "Campus in Autumn", Flickr "Autumn color" 2009, "Dana Porter in October".
- **Winter** (December to March): snow cover from about December to March. Plowed roads and walks with **snow banks**, and salt staining on paths. Bike racks are moved or left un-cleared (see `../bike-racks/` winter_handing).
- **Early spring** (April): leafless trees, brown lawns and mud, geese nesting. The April 2023 walking tour (Earth Walker) shows this, so don't use it for the summer look.
