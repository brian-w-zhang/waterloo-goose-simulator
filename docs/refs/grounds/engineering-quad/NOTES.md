# Engineering precinct (E1-E3, CPH, DWE, E5-E7) courtyards and grounds

## Facts
- **E5** (2010; Shore Tilbe Irwin + Partners) and **E7 / Pearl Sullivan Engineering Building** (2018; Perkins&Will with Smith + Andersen engineering).
  - They are linked by a **seven-storey atrium** with bridges at every level.
  - E7 is linked to E5 and E6 by elevated bridges. The **E7 bridge spans Ring Road** (UW News landmark photo `000_e7-bridge.jpg`).
  - E5 has an exterior **footbridge toward the station / Phillip St side** (UW Plant Ops layer "Engineering 5 footbridge").
  - E5 is on the **east side of Ring Road**, between Ring Road and the ION tracks / Laurel Trail.
- **CPH (Carl A. Pollock Hall) and DWE (Douglas Wright Engineering):**
  - The **CPH outside seating area** (Commons `003_*`): a paved area with tables beside the brown-brick and concrete buildings.
  - A **bike fix-it station with a pump on the north side of DWE**, by room 1417, adjacent to CPH (UW cycling FAQ).
  - The "**Mech Eng secret courtyard**", entered from East Ring Road (Flickr, `../ring-road/private/web/010_*`): an enclosed courtyard of grey interlocking pavers, a mature red-leaf Japanese maple, and **orange/yellow-painted door or panel reveals** in the opening. This is probably the E3 / E1 courtyard.
- **E2 pond / fountain:** the "Engineering 2 Fountain" (-80.54022, 43.47067) and a small pond where geese swim (Flickr "Geese Pond"). Also the **C.J. Moore memorial bench** (-80.54045, 43.47051) and the **Steel Construction Teaching Aid** sculpture (-80.54109, 43.47083).
- **Early Morning Dream** (Sadowska, 1972) at (-80.53915, 43.47049), on the east side.
- East Ring Road traffic calming and **pedestrian crossovers** (2015-2016), between the E-buildings and the ION tracks. See `../ring-road/`.
- I found no published landscape plan for the E1-E3 / CPH / DWE courtyards. Use Street View and Earth captures.
