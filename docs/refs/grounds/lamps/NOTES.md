# Campus light standards

## Data
- **OSM:** 416 `highway=street_lamp` nodes inside the boundary (`private/data/osm_in_boundary.geojson`).
  - About 73 of these carry power-pole tags (material wood 48, concrete 25), so they are utility poles with luminaires.
  - The rest are untyped: no height or fixture tags.
  - They are still useful for **positions and spacing** along Ring Road and the main paths.
- **No UW lighting layer** exists. The Plant Ops campus dataset list does not mention lighting either.
- The City of Waterloo converted its streetlights to LED (Énergère partnership), but there is no public City streetlight layer. That conversion affects only the edge streets: Columbia, Westmount, Hagey.

## Types seen in photos (estimates; verify against Street View)
1. **Ring Road and lot poles:**
   - Tall (about 8-10 m) **galvanised or silver round tapered poles**.
   - A single **curved or upswept arm** carrying an LED cobra or shoebox head (`../ring-road/private/web/009_*` night shot).
   - Lots often have double-arm poles.
   - Some older **elliptical-head** poles (DC east side, 2014 photo, per `../grounds-commons/NOTES.md`).
2. **Pedestrian path lamps (most common on the inner campus):**
   - **Black** round posts about 4-5 m tall, with a flat disc or cap head, or a short conical cap (MC, QNC plaza, rock garden, DC plaza).
   - A tall black variant with a flat round head appears on the DC plaza (2025).
3. **Post-top lantern / acorn lamps on lawns:**
   - Black post with a white globe or lantern head, about 3.5-4 m. See `private/web/002_*`, a willow at night with a post-top lamp.
   - Location uncertain. The photo is captioned only "campus at night"; it may be near Laurel Creek or Columbia Lake.
4. **Wall packs and building-mounted floods** on most buildings, and **bollard lights** at some plazas (Arts Quad lighting design 2018; QNC). Not documented here.
5. **Utility poles with cobra heads:** wood or concrete distribution poles on the north campus (Columbia St, Phillip St side), per the OSM power tags.
