# Laurel Creek and the Laurel Trail

## Laurel Creek
- **Course through campus:** the creek enters from the north-west (Laurel Creek Reservoir / Conservation Area, off campus). It runs through **Columbia Lake** (north campus), past Village 1 / Ring Road, through the **Health Services pond ("Strauss Lake")** and **Laurel Lake** (south campus), and leaves south-east toward Waterloo Park / Silver Lake.
- **Data:**
  - City `WaterFeatures_Creeks`: 23 segments in the bbox; Laurel Creek perennial 12, intermittent 6; plus Clair Creek. Saved as `private/data/city_creeks_bbox.geojson`.
  - EcoMap Creeks: 21; "Laurel Creek" major, managed by Plant Ops.
  - OSM waterways are in `../maps/private/data/uw_waterways.geojson`.
- **Character:**
  - A small channel about 3-6 m wide.
  - Naturalised banks in the **UW Urban Forest** (about 5 ha woodland between main campus and the colleges; designated a National Healing Forest).
  - Marshy floodplain, willows, dogwood, cattails; buckthorn understorey being removed.
  - **Timber and steel footbridges** cross it (see the photosphere "Bridge to University Colleges", -80.5443, 43.4683). City bridges layer: a Waterloo Park West pedestrian bridge 15 m long x 1.5 m wide (concrete, 1967) and a steel bridge 9 m x 2.6 m. Those are off campus but similar in kind.
  - Heritage attribute in the City study: "network of foot paths and bridges ... Laurel Creek".
  - 1976 photo: O-Week tug-of-war across the creek.

## Laurel Trail (Great Trail / Trans Canada Trail / Waterloop)
- City `Trails_and_Pathways` in the bbox (`private/data/city_trails_bbox.geojson`, 163 segments):
  - "Trans Canada Trail / Laurel Trail / Waterloop": **asphalt**, with some **stonedust** and concrete segments.
  - "**University of Waterloo Trail**": 17 asphalt segments, owned by UW.
  - "Waterloo Park West/East", the West Connector (stonedust), Hagey Blvd and Wes Graham Way multi-use paths.
  - EcoMap Trails: 65 multi-use and 31 community trail segments. Surface: asphalt 67, concrete 16.
- The Laurel Trail runs **beside the ION / CN Waterloo Spur corridor** on the east side of campus. It crosses the tracks at grade at UW Station, then continues south through Waterloo Park (iron bridge) toward Uptown.
- **Width** about 3.0 m of asphalt with grass shoulders. There are **green Trans Canada / Great Trail marker posts**; Flickr "Laurel bike trail" shows a round trail marker on a timber post.
