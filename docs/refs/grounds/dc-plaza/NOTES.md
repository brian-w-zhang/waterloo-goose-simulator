# DC plaza (Davis Centre surroundings)

## Facts
- **Davis Centre (William G. Davis Computer Research Centre):** completed 1988 and RAIC award-winning; a pioneering "green" building (City heritage study).
  - Long low building with a **glazed barrel-vault roof**, **red-painted steel portal frames / colonnade**, large round green-painted ventilation ducts, and white precast or metal-panel walls.
- **Approach from Ring Road** (east side, near E3 / E5): a paved forecourt and plaza.
  - Paving is grey interlocking concrete pavers in herringbone or running bond (per `../grounds-commons/NOTES.md`).
  - **Square tapered exposed-aggregate concrete planters**.
  - Tall black lamp posts with flat round heads (2025).
  - **Large bike-rack banks** along the walls: galvanised inverted-U on rails, and red wave racks in 2012. The inventory lists "DC outside entrance 1842G" (see `../bike-racks/`).
  - A canopy over the entrance doors.
- **Davis Centre Road service lot:** 12 stalls (UW lot polygons).
- The green to the west is in `../mc-dc-green/`.
