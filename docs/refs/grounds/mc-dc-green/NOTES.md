# MC / DC green ("MC green") and the B1/B2 green

## What and where
- **"MC green":** the open lawn between **MC (Mathematics & Computer)**, **DC (Davis Centre)** and **M3**, north of MC and west of DC. The Commons panorama (2007) is taken from MC green looking south, with DC on the left and MC, SLC and PAC on the right.
- The **M3 overpass**, an enclosed bridge between MC, M3 and DC, crosses the area (Commons "M3 Overpass"; the photosphere "Bridge between MC, M3 and DC" at -80.54407, 43.47281).
- **History:** the "**B1 / B2 green**" was a larger field south-east of MC. It became a construction site in the late 2000s ("dig it: requiem for a green", Flickr 2006-2009), for QNC (2012) and the rock-garden edge. MC green itself shrank when **M3** (2018) was built on part of it.
  - Older photos (2005-2009) therefore **show more lawn than exists today**. Check against Earth and Street View captures.

## Look (2007 panorama, `../grounds-commons/NOTES.md` and photos here)
- A **wide, gently rolling mown lawn**.
- Young street trees in loose rows, and a **cluster of tall spruces between MC and SLC**.
- Concrete walkways about 2-3 m wide cross it on desire lines.
- A service lane with parked Plant Ops pickups.
- Black lamp posts.
- MC is brutalist board-formed concrete on pilotis; DC is the low glazed "greenhouse" vault with **red steel frames** and big green ducts.
- In summer the lawn is the main lounging, frisbee and goose-grazing field.
