## Named gardens (EcoMap descriptions; OSM centres in lon/lat)
- **Robert S. Dorney Ecology Garden** (1988): native Southern Ontario perennials, at the front of EV1 across from ML (-80.54282, 43.4684). Ecology Lab.
- **Arts/Environment Gardens** (-80.54225, 43.46775), a set of theme beds between EV1, EV2 and Hagey Hall:
  - Xeriscape (-80.54203, 43.46761).
  - Native (-80.54222, 43.46767).
  - Zen (-80.54233, 43.46761).
  - Wind (-80.54228, 43.46793).
  - Spring bulb (-80.5424, 43.46806).
  - Butterfly (-80.54234, 43.468).
  - Fern & Moss (-80.54232, 43.46742).
  - Volunteers maintain them weekly in season; the contractor Quiet Nature visits twice a year.
- **Woodland Fern Garden:** between PAS and EV2 (-80.54266, 43.46794). Shady; a work in progress (2021).
- **Prairie Savanna:** south side of EV1. Native grasses and wildflowers.
- **Constructed wetland:** beside EV3. It takes stormwater from the EV2/EV3 roofs and lots.
- **Peter Russell Rock Garden:** see that folder.
- **Green roofs:**
  - EV3 (1400 m², natural / meadow).
  - QNC, EIT, E5, Hagey Hall extension, Conrad Grebel (sedum).
- Food Services and the Indigenous Student Centre also have gardens (EcoMap ManagedBy values).
- Several small unnamed gardens around South Campus Hall / Needles Hall (-80.5415 to -80.5418, 43.4693-43.4695) and a retaining-wall garden at -80.54227, 43.46906 (ML side).
