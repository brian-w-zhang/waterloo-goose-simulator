# "Strauss Lake" and the campus ponds

## Name check
- **OSM** (and the UW `Water_and_Greenspace` layer derived from it) names a small pond **"Strauss Lake"**: centre (-80.54577, 43.47088), about **76 x 49 m**, natural=water / water=lake.
- That position is the pond **in front of Health Services**, west of SLC / Ring Road near PAC. UW sources call it the **"Health Services pond"** (1999 / 2004 Bulletins) or the "reflecting pond by Health and Safety". The ArcGIS StoryMap campus tour has a stop called "Health Services Pond Reservoir".
- **No UW or City source uses "Strauss Lake".** Treat it as an OSM / local name and label it "Health Services pond" unless confirmed. It is a man-made reservoir on Laurel Creek.

## Other ponds (OSM / City; lon/lat)
- **Pond outside Engineering 2** (Flickr "Geese Pond"): small. OSM shows tiny water polygons near (-80.5403, 43.4707), next to the "Engineering 2 Fountain" (-80.54022, 43.47067).
- **Pond near the UW Station / Laurel Trail:** (-80.54109, 43.47347), about 34 x 49 m. A blog notes a great blue heron at "a pond next to the Laurel Trail just south of the LRT stop".
- **Stormwater retention ponds north of Columbia St** (R&T Park / Lot X area): three ponds at about (-80.5453, 43.4791), (-80.5461, 43.4788) and (-80.5456, 43.4797). City: a pond of 13,074 m² at (-80.54564, 43.47909).
- **Laurel Lake SWM pond:** (-80.54371, 43.46548).
- **North campus / CLV ponds:** (-80.5544, 43.4774), 9,275 m² (City); (-80.5558, 43.4767); (-80.5582, 43.4743), about 124 x 190 m (near Columbia Lake); (-80.5613, 43.4715), 1,365 m² (CLV).
- **Constructed wetland** beside EV3 (EcoMap).
- Polygons: `../maps/private/data/uw_waterbodies.geojson` (OSM) and `city_waterbodies_bbox.geojson`.
