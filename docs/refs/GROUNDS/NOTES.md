# GROUNDS (paths, plazas, furniture, plants around MC / DC / QNC / SLC): visual reference notes

There are 6 files here, plus ground-level detail in the building folders. Dates run from 2005 to 2012, so there is nothing recent. Locations are only as good as the uploader captions and Flickr geotags. Inferred locations are flagged.

## Files
- `GROUNDS_MC_green_panorama_looking_south_2007.jpg`: from MC Green looking south. The uploader identifies DC (left), MC, SLC and PAC (right).
  - A wide open mown lawn, gently rolling.
  - Young street trees in a loose row, and a cluster of tall spruces between MC and SLC.
  - Concrete walkways crossing the lawn.
  - Service pickups and a trailer parked at the left edge on a paved lane.
  - A black lamp post.
- `GROUNDS_rock_garden_path_MC_QNC_2012.jpg` (caption: "between MC and Nano"): a curving **grey asphalt path** about 2 m wide, with a fork.
  - Large flat-topped granite boulders on mulch, one with small bronze plaques.
  - A grey stone, and daffodils.
  - Low spreading junipers and yews, mature pines, and deciduous trees.
  - A black lamp post with a cap head.
  - Behind: a heavy timber pergola, a buff brick 3-storey building (probably C2, or the B/ESC complex; unconfirmed), and MC's pilotis ground floor through the trees.
- `GROUNDS_pergola_wisteria_garden_2009.jpg` (location inferred, probably the same rock-garden area next to MC):
  - **Heavy rough-sawn timber pergola**: square posts, knee braces, double beams, thick twisting wisteria trunks.
  - Large **cracked concrete slab** paving, with a red-brown paver edge at right.
  - Several **cylindrical stone column fragments** (salvaged or sculptural) standing upright.
  - Low timber block benches and rocks.
  - In the background: buff brick and a concrete building on pilotis (MC-like).
- `GROUNDS_poster_kiosk_benches_walkway_2009.jpg` (location inferred):
  - A gravel/asphalt path through junipers and maples to a small plaza.
  - A **round poster kiosk** covered in flyers with a green/grey conical cap, on a round base with a curved timber bench.
  - Straight timber benches on concrete legs on either side.
  - A concrete plaza beyond, and a building on pilotis (MC-like).
  - The same kiosk type appears in `../QNC/QNC_honeycomb_cube_east_face_from_plaza_2011.jpg`, which suggests the MC/QNC/rock-garden plaza.
- `GROUNDS_egg_fountain_sculpture_2011.jpg`: a **bronze cracked-egg fountain**, about 1.5 m tall, dark bronze with blue-green patina, water spilling from the crack into a shallow square concrete pool. Behind it: a brown/buff brick building with ribbon windows and a concrete-faced ground storey, and a red-brown paver walk. The Flickr geotag (43.471744, -80.543478) is at the MC/C2 junction; the location is not confirmed otherwise.
- `GROUNDS_MC_rock_garden_dead_leaves_2005.jpg`: a plant close-up only. Dry, papery, golden-brown leaves held on a twiggy shrub in the "Math & Computer rock garden"; they look like retained beech or hornbeam foliage.

## Recurring street furniture and surfaces (from all folders)
- **Lamp posts.**
  - Pedestrian lamps: slim **black** round posts with a small flat/conical cap or disc head (MC, QNC plaza, rock garden, DC plaza).
  - Road/plaza lamps: grey/silver poles with a **curved arm and elliptical head** (DC east side, 2014).
  - Tall black posts with a flat round head (DC plaza, 2025).
- **Paving.**
  - Grey interlocking concrete pavers in herringbone or running bond (DC plaza and walkways).
  - Small square grey/buff concrete pavers (QNC east plaza, MC entrance).
  - Light grey square pavers with charcoal paver bands (SLC courtyard).
  - Red-brown interlocking pavers on older walks (MC west, DC 2006).
  - Asphalt in the rock garden paths.
  - Large cracked concrete slabs under the pergola.
  - Plain concrete sidewalks along Ring Road.
- **Bike racks.**
  - Galvanised inverted-U hoops on a ground rail (DC 2025).
  - Red wave racks (DC 2012).
  - Galvanised coil/grid racks (SLC 2020).
  - Bikes also heavily parked along DC walls (all years).
- **Benches and tables.**
  - Timber-slat benches on concrete feet (SLC pergola).
  - Grey slatted benches (DC walkway 2016).
  - Concrete bench blocks (DC east).
  - Maroon metal picnic tables (SLC 2007), later timber-topped picnic tables.
  - Circular kiosk benches.
- **Planters.**
  - Square tapered exposed-aggregate concrete planters (DC plaza).
  - Round raised concrete planter with a tree (SLC courtyard).
  - Low round concrete planter curbs (QNC plaza).
- **Bins.** Square concrete or grey metal litter bins, and a black 3-stream waste/recycling station (DC, 2025).
- **Signs.** Black wayfinding post with white blade signs (MC west, 2002). Low monument signs on concrete plinths (Ring Road by QNC/SLC, 2013).
- **Trees and plants.**
  - Maples (several species; orange/red in autumn), horse-chestnut (flowering, at MC), honey locust-like small trees (SLC).
  - Purple-leaf ornamental trees (DC 2006).
  - Tall blue/white spruces and pines clustered near DC, MC and SLC; Lombardy-poplar-like columnar trees (DC plaza edge).
  - Low junipers and yews in the rock garden; wisteria on the pergola.
  - Ornamental grasses in beds (MC 2004); annual flower beds at path forks (MC Green 2007).
- **Ring Road.** Two-lane asphalt with a yellow centre line, concrete curbs, a striped lane and a GRT bus shelter (dark frame, glass) near QNC (2010).
